METHODOLOGY

Phase 1 – Querying the ArCo Knowledge Graph

We used SPARQL to progressively search the ArCo knowledge graph for information related to the Sanremo Music Festival. As no relevant data were found, we decided to create the required knowledge from scratch.

Phase 2 – Prompt Engineering for LLMs

We used zero-shot, chain-of-thought, and few-shot prompting with ChatGPT, Gemini and Mistral, comparing their outputs for accuracy, completeness, and adherence to the prompts. Gemini was selected as the most reliable overall source.

Phase 3 – RDF/OWL and Turtle Representation

We transformed the validated information into 159 RDF triples using Turtle and OWL, representing the festival, its 2026 edition, participants, songs, hosts, and winner through existing properties and links from ArCo and Wikidata.

Phase 4 – SPARQL CONSTRUCT

We converted the Turtle data into a SPARQL CONSTRUCT query to generate an RDF graph containing the new information and integrate it into the ArCo knowledge graph.

Phase 5 – Website Creation and Publication

Github Pages was employed to develop an online platform through which the project could be presented in an organised and user-friendly manner. The resulting website was made publicly available as an open educational resource to support the exploration and reuse of the generated content.
