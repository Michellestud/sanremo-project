SPARQL CONSTRUCT

For the purpose of our project, we aimed to integrate the new information retrieved and represented in the previous step into the ArCo Knowledge Graph. With this objective in mind, we employed the CONSTRUCT query form in SPARQL, as it enables RDF data to be transformed into sets of triple templates, simulating its addition to the ArCo database. In fact, unlike SELECT, which returns a table of results taken from the cultural heritage knowledge graph, CONSTRUCT generates a single RDF graph containing the information we provide.

We used the same code previously developed in Turtle format, but with two main differences:

  • the prefix labels are written differently to be supported by SPARQL.
  • the open class of participants. As a matter of fact, we replaced the owl:Restriction with owl:oneOf to go from a closed class to an open list of participants to make the query readable by ArCo. As a result, the list of contestants in the now-concluded Sanremo Music Festival 2026 is represented as an open class, unlike the closed list produced in our RDF/OWL Turtle triples.

Finally, it is important to note that the WHERE clause remains empty because the purpose of the query is to insert new triples into the ArCo semantic knowledge graph rather than to retrieve already existing data.
