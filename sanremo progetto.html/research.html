RESEARCH

This project aims to investigate whether the Sanremo Music Festival is represented as a cultural or historical-artistic property within ArCo (Architecture of Knowledge), the knowledge graph dedicated to Italian cultural heritage developed by the Italian Ministry of Culture.

Based on the findings, our research work will subsequently explore the possibility of enriching the knowledge graph with information about the Italian festival.

The process of retrieving data will involve querying Large Language Models (LLMs) using three different prompting strategies in order to obtain and compare relevant information about the chosen topic. The knowledge retrieved would later be represented as RDF triples using the Turtle syntax.

Finally, we will simulate the incorporation of said triples into the ArCo knowledge graph through a dedicated SPARQL CONSTRUCT query, demonstrating how LLM-generated information can be transformed into structured data and integrated into an existing cultural heritage knowledge graph.
